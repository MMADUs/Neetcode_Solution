An anagram is a word or phrase made by rearranging the letters of another word or phrase. You must use all the original letters exactly once. 

[1] (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anagram), 

Common Examples:

- Listen -> Silent
- Dormitory -> Dirty room
- Secure -> Rescue
- Tom Marvolo Riddle -> I am Lord Voldemort (from Harry Potter)
